              
## Seats 
Plan your event seating in one place. Add guests, drag them to tables, import your list from a CSV, and use “like” and “dislike” groups so people sit with who they want (and not with who they don’t). 

Auto-assign to fill or balance tables, then share your plan. No more spreadsheets or sticky notes — just a simple seating planner that actually works.
